<div class="diff">
  <div class="diff-inner">
    <p class="diff-head">The Framework Difference</p>
    <div class="diff-grid">
      <div class="diff-item">
        <div class="diff-icon">
          <svg viewBox="0 0 46 46" xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg">
            <path d="M23 4 L40 11 V22 C40 32 33 39 23 42 C13 39 6 32 6 22 V11 Z" fill="none" stroke="#E0432A" stroke-width="2"/>
            <path d="M15 23 L21 29 L32 16" fill="none" stroke="#E0432A" stroke-width="2.5" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round"/>
          </svg>
        </div>
        <h3>Type Safety</h3>
        <p>Requests and responses are typed end-to-end with <code>Command&lt;T&gt;</code> and <code>CommandHandler&lt;REQ,RES&gt;</code>. The compiler catches shape mismatches before CI does.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="diff-item">
        <div class="diff-icon">
          <svg viewBox="0 0 46 46" xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg">
            <path d="M6 8 H40 L26 24 V36 L20 33 V24 Z" fill="none" stroke="#1E76A8" stroke-width="2" stroke-linejoin="round"/>
          </svg>
        </div>
        <h3>Validated at the Boundary</h3>
        <p>Jakarta <code>@Valid</code> fires on the request DTO before a handler ever runs — no runtime JSON parsing scattered through services.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="diff-item">
        <div class="diff-icon">
          <svg viewBox="0 0 46 46" xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg">
            <path d="M35 15 A16 16 0 1 0 38 26" fill="none" stroke="#7CB93C" stroke-width="2.5" stroke-linecap="round"/>
            <path d="M38 6 L35 15 L26 13" fill="none" stroke="#7CB93C" stroke-width="2.5" stroke-linejoin="round" stroke-linecap="round"/>
          </svg>
        </div>
        <h3>Resilient by Default</h3>
        <p>Every handler carries a Resilience4j <code>@Retry</code> policy, configured per command in <code>application.properties</code>.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

      <div class="workflow-card">

<!-- ============ ENGINEERING HIGHLIGHTS ============ -->
<section class="section" id="highlights">
  <div class="section-inner">
    <div class="section-head">
      <h2>Engineering Highlights</h2>
      <p>Six patterns established across every one of the fourteen pull requests.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="hl-grid">
      <div class="hl-card">
        <div class="hl-icon">1</div>
        <h3>Typed Command Framework</h3>
        <p>Three generic pieces &mdash; <code>Command&lt;T&gt;</code>, <code>CommandHandler&lt;REQ,RES&gt;</code>, <code>CommandDispatcher</code> &mdash; replace <code>JsonCommand</code> everywhere.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="hl-card">
        <div class="hl-icon">2</div>
        <h3>Jakarta Validation at Boundary</h3>
        <p>Request DTOs carry <code>@NotBlank</code>, <code>@NotNull</code>, <code>@Positive</code>. Validation fires before the handler runs.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="hl-card">
        <div class="hl-icon">3</div>
        <h3>URL-Level Authorization</h3>
        <p>Permission checks move out of business logic into Spring Security's <code>SecurityConfig</code>. Handlers stay focused on domain logic.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="hl-card">
        <div class="hl-icon">4</div>
        <h3>Resilience4j Retry Per Command</h3>
        <p>Every handler carries <code>@Retry(name = "commandXxxYyy")</code> with retry parameters in <code>application.properties</code>.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="hl-card">
        <div class="hl-icon">5</div>
        <h3>Handler Unit Tests</h3>
        <p>Mockito-based tests for every handler assert dispatch semantics and service delegation &mdash; no integration setup required.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="hl-card">
        <div class="hl-icon">6</div>
        <h3>Legacy Removal</h3>
        <p>Every migration <em>deletes</em> the corresponding <code>CommandWrapperBuilder.xxx()</code> and <code>NewCommandSourceHandler</code>.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
