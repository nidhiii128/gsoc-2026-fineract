== About Apache Fineract

image::fineract-logo.svg[Apache Fineract, 320, align=left]

Apache Fineract is a multi-tenant core-banking platform under the Apache Software Foundation. It powers microfinance institutions, digital banks, and fintechs reaching over 500 million people across Africa, Asia, and Latin America.

Under the hood: Java, Spring Boot, JPA, 200+ REST endpoints, integration tests across Postgres, MySQL, and MariaDB, and a Feign client auto-generated from OpenAPI.
